## 📌 Project Overview

SecureX Payment is an **AI enabled escrow wallet system** designed to solve trust issues in SME transactions.

It ensures that payments are securely held in escrow until both parties fulfill agreed terms, reducing fraud, disputes, and payment delays.

# 🚨 Problem Statement

Small and medium sized businesses (SMEs) face a major trust gap when handling payments.

Customers fear:
- Paying for services that may not be delivered
- Fraud or unreliable sellers

SMEs struggle with:
- Delayed payments after delivery
- Lack of structured payment protection systems
- Disputes with customers

This leads to:
> ❌ Loss of trust  
> ❌ Payment disputes  
> ❌ Reduced digital transactions  

---

# 🎯 Project Goals

- Build a secure escrow based payment system
- Increase trust between buyers and sellers
- Provide transparent transaction tracking
- Reduce payment disputes
- Introduce AI assisted trust monitoring

---

# 🔍 Research & Insights

### 💡 Key Findings

- Users prioritize **trust over speed**
- Many SME transactions rely on informal agreements
- Lack of visibility causes most disputes
- Users want confirmation before releasing funds

---

### ⚠️ Pain Points

**Customers**
- Fear of scams
- No guarantee of refund
- No visibility of transaction progress

**SMEs**
- Late payments
- Lack of proof of delivery disputes
- Weak trust systems
